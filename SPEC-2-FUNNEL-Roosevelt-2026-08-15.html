How this relates to the brand specification

You have received two documents. They do different things and one partly overrides the other, so please read this before either.

Specification 1, the brand and build spec, asked you not to alter section order, CTA count or CTA wording. That instruction was written to protect the conversion structure while we changed colour, type and imagery. Everything in that document about brand tokens, the P0 build defects and the missing content still stands in full and is unchanged.

This document supersedes only the funnel-lock portion of it. We are now deliberately restructuring the funnel, which changes section order, adds a gate ahead of the video, and changes the CTA sequence. Where the two documents disagree on funnel structure, this one wins. Where they touch anything else, specification 1 wins.

Order of work is unchanged: the P0 items in specification 1 come first, starting with admin provisioning, because nothing else can be populated until that is working.

01

The funnel

Four steps, three exits. The contact is captured before the video rather than after it.

00

The gate

Landing state. Headline, one line of proof, and a two-field form. No video visible, no navigation, no pricing. The only available action is to enter a name and email.

01

The VSL

Unlocks on submit. Full video explaining the offer end to end. One CTA beneath it, revealed at a set watch percentage rather than on load.

02

The qualifier

Eight questions, one per screen, progress bar visible throughout. Goal and situation first, money last.

03

The routing

Route computed on submit. One of three outcome screens renders. No dead ends.

High

Qualifies for one-to-one. Straight to the calendar. Tier and price are confirmed on the call, never on the page.

Low

Not right for one-to-one today. Directed to the 8-Week Challenge as the correct starting point.

Nurture

Not ready to invest. Directed into a free WhatsApp group.

Two fields, not three. Every field at a gate costs opt-ins, and a phone number has no obvious justification before the visitor has watched anything. It is asked later, where the reason is self-evident, which should give us the same data at a materially better completion rate.

Build the gate so it can be switched off. Gating reduces the number of people who reach the video, in exchange for capturing every one who passes through. That is the right trade for an application funnel, but we want to measure it rather than assume it. Please make the gate a configurable toggle in the admin so we can run gated against ungated as a split test once the page is live, without a redeploy.

The escape hatch matters more than it looks. Without it, anyone who realises early that coaching is out of reach simply closes the tab and is lost. With it, they self-select into the free group and stay in the ecosystem. It costs a small number of applications and recovers a much larger number of contacts.

04

Step 02: the qualifier

Eight questions, one per screen, progress bar throughout. The order is deliberate. By the time the investment question appears the visitor has answered six things and is invested in finishing. Please do not reorder them.

Question 1 Routing: none
What are you trying to change?
  • Lose fat
  • Build muscle
  • Get stronger and fitter
  • Rebuild after a break or injury
Purpose: Opens with the easiest possible question. Does not route. Feeds the call brief and the follow-up segmentation.
Question 2 Routing: none
Where are you starting from?
  • I train consistently already
  • I train on and off
  • I have stopped completely
  • I have never trained properly
Purpose: Establishes the gap the coaching closes. Feeds the call brief.
Question 3 Routing: conditional
Realistically, how many days a week can you train?
  • Five or more
  • Three to four
  • Two
  • Fewer than two
Purpose: "Fewer than two" sets a flag used by routing rule 4 below. It does not disqualify on its own.
Question 4 Routing: none
What has got in the way before? Choose any that apply.
  • Staying consistent
  • Not knowing what to do
  • Nutrition
  • Time
  • Injury
Purpose: Multi-select. Store as an array. Whatever is selected is what the call opens with.
Question 5 Routing: hard
How soon do you want to start?
  • Immediately
  • Within two weeks
  • Within a month
  • Just looking for now
Purpose: "Just looking" routes to nurture regardless of the investment answer.
Question 6 Routing: none
Have you worked with a coach before?
  • Yes, I have one now
  • Yes, in the past
  • Never
Purpose: Determines which of three very different calls Kane runs.
Question 7 Routing: primary
Coaching with Kane directly starts in the hundreds per month. If we agree it is the right fit, what are you ready to put into getting this right?
  • £1,000 or more
  • £500 to £1,000
  • £150 to £500
  • Under £150 right now
Purpose: The primary routing question, deliberately last. Framed as readiness to invest rather than ability to pay, banded rather than exact, and stated after price context. Expect this to be the largest drop-off point in the form. That is intended.
Question 8 Routing: none
Last thing. Where should we reach you?
  • Mobile number, with country code
Purpose: Label the screen so the reason is explicit: this is where the call confirmation or the group invite is sent.
05

Routing logic

Evaluated top to bottom. First match wins. All threshold values should be admin-editable.

#ConditionRouteDestination
1Q7 = "Under £150"NurtureFree WhatsApp group
2Q5 = "Just looking"NurtureFree WhatsApp group
3Q7 = "£150 to £500"Low8-Week Challenge
4Q7 ≥ "£500" AND Q3 = "Fewer than two"Low8-Week Challenge
5Q7 = "£500 to £1,000"HighCalendar, Pro track
6Q7 = "£1,000 or more"HighCalendar, Elite track

Rule 4 is a deliberate choice and we would welcome your view on it. Somebody with the budget but training fewer than two days a week cannot get the result the programme promises, so we route them to the Challenge instead. It protects the client and the completion rate at the cost of some one-to-one revenue. If you think that is the wrong call for conversion, say so before you build it.

06

The three outcome screens

Copy below is final. Please implement it as written. The two non-qualifying screens matter more than the qualifying one, because that is where the brand is either respected or resented.

High  Qualifies for one-to-one
You qualify, [First name]. Book your call.

Kane reviews every application himself and will be briefed on your answers before you join. Pick a time that works. Twenty minutes, no pressure, and a straight answer either way on whether this is right for you.

Choose your time
Low  Directed to the Challenge
One-to-one is not the right move for you yet. This is.

Based on your answers, the 8-Week Challenge is where you should start. It is the same training system and the same Performance Coach in WhatsApp, without the one-to-one price. Build eight weeks of consistency on it, and the one-to-one conversation becomes a very different one.

Start the 8-Week Challenge Join the free group instead
Nurture  Directed to the free group
Not the right time to pay for coaching. Come and train with us anyway.

The free group is where a few thousand people train alongside each other. Sessions, questions answered, and the same standard Kane holds his paying clients to. No cost, no card, and no catch. When you are ready for more, you will already know how we work.

Join the group

The visitor is never told they have failed. The Low screen says one-to-one is not the right move yet and positions the Challenge as the correct next step rather than a consolation prize. Both non-qualifying screens carry a second option, so neither is a dead end. Please keep both buttons on both screens.
